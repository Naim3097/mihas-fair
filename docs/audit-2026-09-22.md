# Mission X — polish audit and plan (22 Sep 2026)

Scope: commit `f8176bd` (main, after the neck fix and the 22 Sep upstream merge). Method: five independent code
reads (rendering, input and hit testing, interface, rules and network, cleanliness and build) against a game-feel
checklist, then the running build played by hand at phone size (375 × 812, dpr 2) and in a narrow desktop pane
(597 × 859, dpr 1.75) with the `?perf` box on. Nothing here was tested on a real phone; `docs/phone-test.md` remains
the protocol for that, and this sweep ends with it.

This is an audit and a plan. Nothing in the code changed for it.

## What must not change

The user-facing rules stay as they are until a new concept arrives. In code, that is:

- **QR scanning**, camera to score: `ui/common.tsx:57-85` (BarcodeDetector, jsQR fallback) → `src/scan.ts:9`
  → `ui/common.tsx:46-54` (`?h=` host, `?b=` beacon, `?l=` swap, `stationId = value.split('.')[0]`) → `api.stamp`
  → `POST /api/stamp` (`server/app.ts:82`) → `Game.stamp` (`server/game.ts:318`). Beacon codes are a static HMAC,
  host codes rotate in 30 s windows with 4 windows of grace. Guards that stay: 5 s minimum between stamps, no
  stamping your own booth, a card before an exhibitor's QR, the hero QR is the mission start and never a stamp,
  exhibitors barred from the mission.
- **Points** (`shared/rules.ts:20-35`): card 200, scan 50, stamp, leave card, swap 50, booth online 100, booth 500,
  referral 10. **Chapters**: three (`rules.ts:55-61`): your card → find Lean X Digital → checkpoints. Checkpoints:
  five (`rules.ts:47`), random from approved booths, never your own.
- Presence pinging every 3 s (`fair/engine.ts:43`): the hologram garbage collector derives its timeout from it.

Two things in that area are wrong today and are reported here for a decision, not changed:

1. **A checkpoint scan can award 0 points.** `server/game.ts:361-376`: a player who virtually stamped a checkpoint
   booth first (the Stamp chip offers it, `src/state.ts:114`) and later scans its real beacon gets the tick but not
   the 50, because the `already` branch skips the award. The comment above it says the opposite. Reachable by any
   visitor. Fix is four lines plus a regression test next to `game.test.ts:81-89`; needs your sign-off because it is
   scoring.
2. **The prize code never reaches a phone.** `Me.ticket` is filled by the server (`game.ts:162`) and rendered
   nowhere; the `'prize'` modal exists in `state.ts:8` and is never set; `'claimed'` is set only by the demo. So the
   500 points and the finish sheet fire only in demo mode. Either the ending is surfaced or it is deleted; that is a
   rules decision.

Related, no sign-off needed: `docs/game-rules.md` still describes five chapters and GPS gating; `shared/rules.ts:43`,
`src/state.ts:118`, `src/ui/App.tsx:134,277` still say "five chapters" in comments. `task.md` asks for three
checkpoints instead of five: one constant.

## Measured

- Phone size, standing at the X: 120 fps · 28–30 draw calls · 96k triangles · dpr 2 (a desktop GPU; the phone
  numbers are what `docs/phone-test.md` is for). Desktop pane: 31–58 calls · 101–156k triangles.
- "Take me there" from the spawn: 31 m in about 8 s, sprinting (4.2 m/s) the whole way.
- Bundle: three.js chunk 568 kB (146 kB gzipped), main 261 kB, jsQR 131 kB lazy, total 1.29 MB of JS; public
  assets 1.8 MB, the body 823 KB of it.
- Per client: `/api/presence` every 3 s, `/api/stations` + `/api/today` every 20 s plus once per tab switch; the
  Swap sheet polls `/api/me` every 4 s; the big screen polls its heaviest endpoint every 4 s with no hidden-tab
  guard. At 1,000 players that is about 333 presence requests per second and 700–1,000 Postgres queries per second.

## Findings

### A. Movement, navigation, camera

1. **Booth taps stop working below roof height.** `src/game/pick.ts:22` bails when the camera is at or under 2.6 m.
   The default camera sits at about 2.76 m; a pinch-in or the wall pull-in inside an aisle takes it lower and every
   tap falls through to walk-to-floor with no feedback.
2. **"Take me there" to the X instead of the next checkpoint** (task.md's bug): `fair/engine.ts:408` uses
   `guideTarget ?? hero.dock`; the mini HUD's Go button never sets a target (`App.tsx:159`), and `updateTrail` clears
   the target once the path is under 6 m (`engine.ts:490`), so later presses go home.
3. **Autopilot sprints and cannot be stopped**: `engine.ts:251` chooses sprint whenever the route is longer than 5 m;
   the only cancel is a stick nudge.
4. **No camera recentre**: `snapBehind` runs once at spawn (`engine.ts:186`). On a phone the player must orbit by
   hand constantly, and stick-forward drifts from body-forward.
5. **Portrait field of view never applies**: `resize()` sets 58°, `CameraRig.update` writes 55° back every frame
   (`src/ceritera/game/camera.ts:62-63`).
6. **Camera through posts and walls**: one ray from the chest (`camera.ts:48-50`), no corner handling. Seen in play:
   an entrance post through the middle of the frame on arrival at the X. The intro's "settle in from above" is a hard
   cut (`engine.ts:186,217`).
7. **Two collision models** (physics boxes for the body, a 0.5 m nav grid for routes) papered over by a stall
   detector that re-plans three times then gives up (`engine.ts:253-259`). `goToBooth` parks you at whichever of four
   front points is nearest, open side ignored (`engine.ts:392-395`).
8. **Touch sprint is one flick away**: full stick push is sprint; the walk band is 0.02–0.45 of the stick.

### B. Hit boxes

9. **Near-booth is a circle to the centre**, 4.4 m client (`engine.ts:443-446`) against 5.0 m on the server. On
   2.5 m aisles it reaches both neighbours and the row behind; nearest-centre wins, so the chip flips, and "at the
   booth" is true behind its back wall. No hysteresis.
10. **No tap tolerance** for fingers in `tapMove` (`engine.ts:374`); the analytic pick is exact to the box.
11. **The picker's depth scale is sampled once at the camera** (`engine.ts:371`) rather than per booth, skewing rays
    that cross the plan's y-scale boundary.
12. **Hover is dead**: `onHover` declared in `fair/input.ts:12`, never wired in the engine, so the desktop blue frame,
    tag and pointer cursor from `docs/design-system.md` do not exist. Confirmed by hand.

### C. Interface and responsiveness

13. **The action row eats the joystick zone**: `.action` is `pointer-events:auto` across its full width at the
    bottom-left (`ui.css:23,99,106`), on top of the touch-stick zone (`input.ts:93`). While a chip or hint shows, the
    first thumb-drag of a session never reaches the canvas.
14. **Targets under 44 px**: chips 36, fold button 28, emote tray items 42, links 32, landscape dock 42
    (`ui.css:43,96,213,229,231`).
15. **Toasts cover the mission card** on entry ("Welcome to MIHAS" sat over the chapter line and the fold button).
16. **Back history desync** (`ui/back.ts:14-16`): closing one sheet and opening another in the same tick pushes then
    pops, so a later Back press leaves the game. Reachable from `App.tsx:66`, `scan.ts:18,23`, `App.tsx:328`.
17. **Labels through the body**: place labels are HTML, so "WELLNESS CORNER" is drawn over the helmet. Their
    occlusion box is a character-count guess (`engine.ts:554`) that ignores real metrics and the `--ui` scale.
18. **Dark-on-dark world labels**: `.lbl` is `--ink-3` with a paper halo (`ui.css:115-116`) over the `#070b16`
    sky; `.lbl.lift` stays blue on near-black. `fair.css` also carries a `backdrop-filter` blur and a gradient that
    `ui.css:2` and the design doc forbid; `fair/palette.ts` has three colours `theme.ts` does not know.
19. **Contrast**: `--ink-3` on white is about 3.2:1 and is used for every `.fine` block at 12.5 px.
20. **Keyboard and focus**: the mini objective card is a `role="button"` with no `onKeyDown` (`App.tsx:155`); sheets
    have no focus trap or restore (`common.tsx:12`). `textarea` at 14 px triggers iOS focus zoom (`ui.css:49`). No
    `visualViewport` handling, so the keyboard can cover card fields (open item in `phone-test.md`).
21. **Map sheet**: no resize or orientation listener (`world-sheets.tsx:24-45`) while taps recompute scale, so taps
    misalign after rotating; at ~0.2 px per metre a booth is sub-pixel.
22. **No desktop layout**: the phone HUD sits in the corners of a wide screen, scaled by `--ui`. Type is fixed px.
23. **Form accessibility**: the card form's inputs have no accessible names (labels are not associated); the
    `booth.tsx:94` "disabled" class has no CSS rule, so a dead link looks live.
24. **Perf box paints over toasts** (z-index 9 inside the stage, dev only).

### D. Rendering and aesthetics

25. **Lighting**: hemisphere 2.3, sun 1.3 at (−80, 140, 60), fill 0.5 (`fair/world.ts:47-49`). No shadow maps anywhere
    in the repo, no environment, no ambient occlusion, no fog. Materials are Lambert (`world.ts:53`,
    `booths.ts:19`); the body is Standard with `envMapIntensity 0`, so it renders nearly Lambert too.
26. **Contact shadow only under bodies** (`fair/nexo.ts:139-141`, a 0.5 m blob). Nothing under counters, towers,
    furniture, gates or the X.
27. **Palette**: `fair/palette.ts` is five near-identical greys on an untextured 204 × 85 m slab. Only approved
    exhibitors with an upload get any texture (`booths.ts:257-275`).
28. **Booth carpets are overwritten**: booth carpet at y 0.012 (`booths.ts:65,77`), hall carpet at 0.02 with
    `depthWrite:false` and `renderOrder 1` covering the whole hall (`world.ts:119,126-127`). The hero's dark carpet
    included. One-line fix.
29. **Depth precision**: camera near 0.1 / far 2600 (`engine.ts:52`), a 26,000:1 ratio, with eight coplanar floor
    layers within 4 cm. Distant carpets and hall lettering will flicker. No fog, so the far wall is fully crisp with
    no depth cue.
30. **Fill rate**: the star dome shader runs full-screen every frame; the four glass panes are double-sided
    transparent boxes, eight blended layers of a 204 m wall (`world.ts:140-145`).
31. **Bodies**: 55k triangles each, 24 joints, `frustumCulled=false` on every body mesh (`nexo.ts:111`), no LOD.
    24 holograms plus the player is 1.4 M triangles and 25 mixers a frame. The lean tier only halves the count.
32. **Per-frame allocations**: a matrix and two vectors per pinned booth per frame (`world.ts:267-268`, up to 256
    pins); two vectors, an array and three closures per frame in `updateLabels` (`engine.ts:544-561`).
33. **Adaptive resolution only ever drops** (`engine.ts:576-584`): 0.25 per 3 s under 42 fps, floor 1.0, never
    recovers. The frame loop never pauses when the tab is hidden or a sheet covers the world.
34. **Redundant work**: the carpet instanced mesh is built twice (`booths.ts:130-132`); fascia and counter meshes
    are found by geometry identity plus instance count, which can pick the wrong part (`booths.ts:133-134`).
35. **Photo** resizes the drawing buffer to 1080 × 1350 and back (`engine.ts:345-353`): two reallocations and a
    one-frame flash risk, and the iOS blank-photo risk from the previous audit is still open.

### E. Stability and network

36. **Unhandled rejections**: the lazy jsQR import (`ui/common.tsx:68`) inside an async IIFE; a failed chunk after a
    deploy kills the camera with no message. `screen.tsx:66` floor data fetch. `screen.tsx:77` two intervals never
    cleared, one dereferencing a scene that may be gone.
37. **No coalescing, backoff or retry** in `net/api.ts:9-20`; polls swallow every error and keep cadence, so a
    failing database keeps full load and the player never sees an offline state. No fetch timeout: a hung
    `floor.json` spins the splash forever.
38. **Request amplification**: `main.tsx:22-31` pulls once per `visibilitychange` with no floor; 1,000 phones on the
    same 20 s interval align.
39. **Server cost**: `ok()` in `app.ts:40` calls `game.me()` on every non-quiet write, about 15 queries with
    `hooks.teamOwner` called twice (`game.ts:147,169`); `/api/today` runs an uncached settings scan plus a per-viewer
    ledger query (`liveops.ts:214-219`); `/api/crew/screen` totals are six uncached counts. Rate limits are
    per-process, useless on Vercel and wrong behind conference NAT.
40. **Client/server drift**: `me` refreshes only on responses that carry it, so checkpoints assigned when the crew
    approves a booth, and the crew's prize dock, stay invisible until the next write. `state.ts:125` uses `scans` for
    both visits and leads on the exhibitor journey.
41. **Stale closures**: `afterCard` is never cleared when the card sheet is dismissed (`App.tsx:66`, `state.ts:70`).

### F. Code cleanliness and build

42. **The fair ships an RPG**: `fair/engine.ts:98` instantiates the whole `src/ceritera/game` `Sim`, so combat,
    enemies, skills and their content ship to every visitor. Movement, controller and camera are the only parts used.
43. **Bundle shape**: one 568 kB three.js chunk, everything 3D eager and `modulepreload`ed before the start screen;
    only jsQR is split.
44. **Dead or stale**: `server/db/d1.ts` (never imported), `shared/avatar.ts` (behind an always-false flag),
    `src/game/world.ts` + `astronaut.ts` + `troupe.ts` (only the big-screen page), `tools/pdf-probe.mjs` and its
    10 MB `pdfjs-dist` devDependency, two one-shot anim tools, about 80 unused exports, `FEATURES` all false with
    ~900 lines of server subsystems reachable only from tests, endpoints with zero client callers (`/api/missions*`,
    `/api/gc*`, `/api/boards`, `/api/sectors`, `/api/trust`, `/api/team*`, `/api/hidden`, `/api/avatar`,
    `/api/ceritera/*`, `/api/host/leads`, `/api/flags`). Load-bearing despite looking dead: `Director`,
    `GroundControl`, `Venue`, `Crews` are wired into `Game.hooks` and run on every stamp.
45. **Dependencies**: `@gltf-transform/core|extensions|functions` used by the tools but undeclared (only the `cli`
    is); `@libsql/client` is a runtime dependency used only by a test helper.
46. **Repository**: `assets-src` is 43 MB of raw art without LFS; the clone is 53 MB. No CI, no ESLint or Prettier;
    style is consistent by hand, but lines run to 459 characters.
47. **Tests**: strong on rules, physics and the demo; nothing on `fair/engine.ts`, `fair/input.ts`, `fair/world.ts`,
    `fair/booths.ts`, the UI, `net/api.ts` or `scan.ts`.

## The plan

Seven phases, in order. Each is one commit or a small series, each ends with the suite green, the typecheck clean,
and a play-through at phone size and desktop; the last phase is the real-phone protocol. Rules-touching items are
marked and wait for your word.

**Phase 1 — Controls and navigation correctness** (findings 1–13)
- Pick floor below roof height, tap tolerance on touch, picker depth scale per booth (`pick.ts`, `engine.ts`).
- Autopilot: explicit target, no fallback to the X, mini HUD gets the same link, run speed not sprint, a Stop button,
  no auto-clear at 6 m; distance nulled with the target.
- Near-booth by footprint with open-side check and hysteresis, kept inside the server's 5 m rule.
- Camera: portrait FOV owned by resize, gentle recentre behind the body while the stick is held on touch, corner-aware
  pull-in, settle-in intro instead of a cut, `goToBooth` by open side.
- Free the joystick zone (`.action` pointer-events), wire hover for desktop (frame, tag, cursor).
- Tests: pick cases at low camera; an input-to-engine test for stick, keys, tap and autopilot on a headless sim.

**Phase 2 — Interface discipline** (findings 14–24)
- All targets to 44 px; toast queue that never covers the mission card; Back history fix in `back.ts`; label
  occlusion from real metrics and `--ui`; dark-theme label styles in `fair.css`; `--ink-3` contrast; keyboard and
  focus on the objective card and sheets; 16 px textarea; `visualViewport` offset for open sheets; map resize
  listener; input labels on the card form; the dead "disabled" class.
- A real desktop layout: objective and action in one band above ~1200 px, type that scales.
- Reconcile `theme.ts`, `fair/palette.ts`, `fair.css` and the design doc into one palette again.
- Tests: a component test per sheet for open, close, Back and Escape.

**Phase 3 — A world with light** (findings 25–35)
- Fix the carpet overdraw first (one line, returns per-stand floor colour).
- Sun shadow map with a bounded orthographic frustum that follows the player, receiving on the slab, casting from
  the booth part meshes, gates, the X and bodies; gated to the high tier; bias tuned against the 12 mm carpets.
- Contact shadow blobs as one instanced mesh under counters, towers, gates and the X.
- Ambient occlusion baked into the hall floor canvas along aisles and partitions.
- Fog and a 0.35 m near plane; single-sided glass; Standard materials at roughness ~0.85 with a small room
  environment on the high tier, Lambert kept on lean.
- Body LOD beyond ~18 m with frustum culling back on; remove the per-frame allocations; adaptive resolution that
  recovers; pause the loop when hidden.
- Measure before and after with `?perf` at phone size: target 60 fps at dpr 2 on the desktop GPU with 24 holograms,
  and no visible flicker on the far carpets.

**Phase 4 — Stability and network** (findings 36–41; two items need sign-off)
- Catch the jsQR import and the big-screen fetch; clear the big-screen timers; hidden-tab guard on its poll.
- `api.ts`: timeout, one retry with jitter for reads, an "offline" chip when polls fail, a 5 s floor on the
  visibility pull, ±20 % jitter on the 20 s interval.
- Server: cache `drop()` and `totals()`, call `teamOwner` once, `leads` from shares not scans, clear `afterCard`.
- Sign-off: refresh `me` on a slow cadence (touches what the mission card shows); any change to the 3 s presence
  interval (the dominant cost at 1,000 players, and the hologram GC depends on it).

**Phase 5 — Code cleanliness and build** (findings 42–47)
- Mechanical: delete `d1.ts`, `pdf-probe.mjs` and `pdfjs-dist`, the one-shot anim tools; declare the gltf-transform
  packages; move `@libsql/client` to dev; strip unused exports; fix the stale content comment.
- Split three.js into its own chunk and load the engine after the start screen paints.
- Split movement, controller and camera out of the RPG `Sim` so the fair bundle drops combat, enemies, skills and
  their content.
- Decide the fate of the big-screen page and its old renderer, the always-false `FEATURES` subsystems and their
  tests, and `assets-src` (LFS or out of the repo).
- CI: `npm run build && npm test` on every push; ESLint and Prettier so this does not drift back.

**Phase 6 — The two rules items, only with your word** (the first section)
- The zero-point checkpoint scan, with a regression test.
- The prize code: surface it or delete the path. Three checkpoints instead of five if you want it now.
- Docs: `game-rules.md` and the "five chapters" comments brought to the three-chapter, no-GPS reality.

**Phase 7 — Proof**
- The `docs/phone-test.md` protocol on a mid-range Android, an iPhone, and the WhatsApp in-app browser, on the
  deployed link with `?perf`. Numbers recorded here. Only after this can the game be called ready.

What I need from you before starting: the order is my recommendation, say if you want the world with light
(Phase 3) first since it is the most visible; and a yes or no on the three sign-off items (the scoring bug, the
prize path, the presence interval).

## Progress

- Picking (`src/game/pick.ts`): the ray is tested from the eye when the camera is under the roofs, the booth the eye
  stands in is never picked, and the picker keeps every booth in the drawn y space it is given (the level-2 stretch

  the server's 5 m rule (asserted in the test). `goToBooth` and the trail end at the open side.
