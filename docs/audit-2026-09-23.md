# Mission X — smoothness sweep and journey audit (23 Sep 2026)

Scope: commit `35d37a7` (main: the Playground, Phases 0–7, on top of the 22 Sep polish sweep). Method: two
independent code reads (the player's journey across both worlds; frame-time and smoothness on a phone) against
the running build, then the build played at phone size (375 × 812) and in the desktop pane with the kits on, the
render counters read per kit, and the whole test suite. Nothing here was tested on a real phone;
`docs/phone-test.md` (rows 1–24) remains the protocol for that.

What was fixed during the sweep is marked **fixed**; what waits for a decision is marked **decide**.

## What must not change

Unchanged since the 22 Sep audit and checked again by diff: QR scanning (`ui/common.tsx` → `src/scan.ts` →
`POST /api/stamp` → `Game.stamp`), the points table and the three chapters in `shared/rules.ts`, five checkpoints,
presence every 3 s. The 18 commits since `f8176bd` touch `server/game.ts` only to ask the team owner once instead
of twice, `shared/rules.ts` only for a comment and two unused constants. The three items that wait for the user's
word still wait: the zero-point checkpoint scan, the prize code, five checkpoints to three.

The Playground's server side (`server/playground.ts`) is additive: its own tables, tokens and caps; the fair's
scoring never reads it.

## Findings

### A. The kits on the body

1. **Skate frames under the boots, the whole skate on the stand.** Nexo's boots are 50 × 34 cm; a skate 33 cm
   long cannot hold one, so the worn piece is the frame and wheels only, cut at the sole
   (`tools/rig/clip-item.mjs --above 0.14`), scale 1.3, the body lifted 11.7 cm. Checked in both worlds from behind
   and from the side: the frames sit under the boots, the wheels' cyan rings glow, the glide pose keeps them flat.
   The skate's own boot, pad and strap are never seen on the body; the stand shows the whole thing. Clean.
2. **The jetpack over the suit's own pack.** The suit carries a 66 cm wide pack of its own; the jetpack (scale
   1.35) rides 48 cm behind the chest joint, embedded 5 cm, its MIHAS face to the camera, the two thruster texels
   glowing at its bottom. Flames only while `Player.thrusting`; the exhaust ribbon starts at its bottom. Clean.
3. **Hold-to-fly from the ground needs a jump first** (`controller.ts:209-215`: thrust is judged only in the air).
   Both interfaces send the jump before the hold (the fair's Fly button and the Playground's button call `jump()`
   then `hold(true)`; Space gives the edge and the hold), so a player never meets this; a script calling
   `hold(true)` alone stays down. By design, noted so no one chases it.
4. **The Playground's button said Jump on the Jetpack** while the fair's says Fly. **fixed**: `src/ui/playground.tsx`
   reads Fly (hold) when the gear is the Jetpack.
5. **Other visitors show no kits.** Presence (`shared/types.ts`, `server/presence.ts`) carries no kit, so a body
   seen from across the hall walks on Boots even when its owner glides on Skates. **decide**: adding an optional
   `kit` to the presence ping and the others' list is a small, backwards-compatible server change, but it is a
   server contract change and the memory rule says the fair's rules and wire stay as they are without the word.
6. **Stand showcases were small from the pad** (a 40 cm skate at 4 m). **fixed**: the skate turns at 1.8×, the
   jetpack at 1.3×, both under their labels.

### B. The journey (fair → Playground → fair)

1. **The Playground's card and Jump button appeared over the fair** for as long as the engine chunk took to
   download: `App.tsx` switched the HUD the instant the world signal flipped, `main.tsx` handed the stage over
   only after the import resolved, so a dead button sat over Hall 8. **fixed**: the HUD waits for the engine's
   controls, and the chunk plus the two stand models are fetched the moment the launch-pad chip appears, so the
   door opens on a built world (B4 below with it).
2. **The phone's Back button out of a run threw the run away**: `ui/back.ts` flipped the world without ending
   the run, so nothing was recorded, no best, no board line, and the next entry nulled it. **fixed**: Back ends
   the run the way the menu does.
3. **Menu → "Back to the fair" mid-run computed a summary nobody saw** (the HUD unmounts on the same tick).
   **fixed**: the leave says it in a toast, "Run over: 1,240 points · 14 ★ banked"; the summary sheet stays for
   the gate and the air running out.
4. **Two kits could stack on the body**: a kit taken off and put back before its model arrived left two pending
   loads that both passed the guard. **fixed**: dressing undresses first.
5. **A button held through the end of a run kept the thrust on**: the button unmounts under the summary, so its
   pointer-up never fires, and Again launched the body off the pad by itself. **fixed**: the pad releases the input.
6. **The button said Jump on the Jetpack, the first-run hint promised an air jump the Jetpack does not have.**
   **fixed**: Fly (hold) on the Jetpack, and the hint says hold to climb, let go to drop.
7. **A kit the server took back went silently** and the chip could name a kit the body no longer wore.
   **fixed**: the fair steps to Boots, tells the store so the chip follows, and says "Back on Boots".
8. **A failed Boards request read as "no finished run today"**. **fixed**: "The boards did not answer. Try again
   in a moment."
9. **The world reset behind the summary sheet** 0.9 s after the gate (the body teleported to the pad, the stars
   popped back) through a 32 % scrim. **fixed**: nothing moves behind the sheet; Again puts the body on the pad.
10. **Copy split between "gear" and "kit"** (the menu and the summary said gear, the fair says kit). **fixed**:
    kits everywhere a player reads it; `gear` stays the code's word.
11. **The first body in the Playground was made one line before the gear was read**, so it wore Boots until
    `enter()` repaired it. **fixed**: the gear first.
12. **Stars from a run that is never posted are credited on the phone and reclaimed on the next visit** (a tab
    closed mid-run: the visibility flush covers most of it; a run under the minimum length the server rejects
    is dropped without a word). Rare after B2; **decide** whether the summary should say "not counted: under
    5 s" for the short case. The client's local balance could also be reconciled rather than overwritten on
    sync, a small store change.
13. **The fair has no visible door to the Playground**: the Playground has a lit disc and a floating "Back to
    the fair"; the fair has a text chip that only exists within 4 m of the hero dock, and a menu entry. **decide**:
    a matching disc and label at the dock (`src/fair/world.ts`) would make the two worlds point at each other.
14. Smaller, noted: the diamond adds 25 ★ but floats only "+300 ◆"; the express tray keeps a "Jump" item beside
    the Fly button (both harmless: a jump is still a hop on the Jetpack); a `localStorage` write per star picked
    up (cheap, but a write mid-run).

### C. Smoothness and frame time

1. **Every body cost three skinned passes and a full retargeting of the clip library** (21 clips, 57,000
   keyframes) at construction, on every stranger who walked up and for all of them at once when the model file
   arrived. **fixed**: the rest bounds, the culling spheres and the retargeted clips are measured once per model
   file and shared by every body cut from it (`SHARED` in `src/fair/nexo.ts`); the second bounds pass was
   arithmetic all along.
2. **Disposing any body threw away the geometry every other body was using** (the skeleton clone shares it), so
   a stranger walking out of range forced a re-upload of the mascot's buffers. **fixed**: a body disposes its own
   materials and blob, never the file's geometry.
3. **Meshopt decodes on the main thread** for the body and the clip library at boot. **Tried and reverted**: the
   sweep turned on one decoder worker, which works in the dev server and breaks the built app. three's copy of the
   decoder writes its worker's source with the literal name `workerProcess`, the production minifier renames that
   function, the worker dies on load and every decode waits for an answer that never comes, so the body and the
   clip library never arrived (found running the build live, 23 Sep). The decode stays on the main thread; a
   comment in `src/fair/nexo.ts` says why. **plan**: if the boot cost matters, the decoder from the `meshoptimizer`
   package with its own worker, checked in the built app.
4. **The Playground fetched its stand models at the worst moment** (inside the world's constructor, on first
   entry). **fixed** with B1: fetched at the launch pad.
5. **The worn skate frame was a second file with the same 1024² paint embedded** (368 KB more to download, ~11 MB
   more on the GPU while the stands showed the whole skate). **fixed**: the frame is cut from `skate.glb` at
   load (`src/fair/clip.ts`: a new index over the file's own vertex buffers, tested), one file, one paint.
6. **The kits and the showcases cast shadows on every tier**: the shared source scene carried the flag, and the
   body's per-distance shadow switch never reached its attachments. **fixed**: the kit follows the body's shadow
   state, the two static showcases never enter the shadow map.
7. **Deep bone chains were brought up to date twice per frame** (an explicit update before a call that updates
   anyway) and **the head's attention inverted the root matrix twice**. **fixed**: once each.
8. **The fair rebuilt every booth label atlas on every 20 s station poll**, changed or not, and kept doing it
   while the Playground had the stage (a multi-hundred-millisecond stall that could land mid-run). **fixed**:
   the names rebuild only when a name changed, and the fair takes a booth change on resume rather than behind
   another world.
9. **A new state object per body per frame** in the animation mapping and a new object per airborne raycast.
   **fixed** for the mapping (one object rewritten); the raycast and the plan-to-world helper (`toWorld`
   returns a fresh vector per call, used per guide dot, per pinned booth and per hologram each frame) remain:
   a few hundred short-lived objects a frame, a minor GC every few seconds. **plan**: an out-parameter on
   `toWorld`/`toPlan` and scratch vectors at the call sites (`src/fair/engine.ts:581, 625, 673`,
   `src/fair/world.ts:229`, `src/playground/engine.ts:178`).
10. **Texture memory** is the phone's real ceiling: every texture is uncompressed WebP → RGBA8 in GPU memory,
    ~48 MB for the body (the 2048² LED emissive map is 1 KB compressed and 21 MB resident), ~32 MB for the LOD
    body loaded alongside it on the phone tier, ~11 MB per kit. **plan**: KTX2/ETC2 textures (≈8× smaller
    resident, needs the transcoder), the LED map at 512², and one mascot file per tier rather than both.
11. **The quality ladder recompiles every shader when it toggles shadows** at level 2↔3, exactly when the phone is
    already slow, and can bounce back after 8 s. **plan**: precompile both variants at load
    (`renderer.compile`) or widen the hysteresis so the crossing happens once.
12. **Two skies, two lights**: the still is fetched and uploaded twice, and on the non-lean tier both worlds keep
    a video decoding. **plan**: one shared sky, the hidden world's video paused in `Stage.use`.
13. **The fair steps its sim at the frame's dt while the Playground steps at a fixed 1/60**, so the same kit feels
    different at 30 fps in the fair. **decide**: the accumulator in the fair too is a one-line change with a
    feel change behind it; not done in a sweep.
14. Clean: no frame-rate-dependent damping anywhere (every ease is `damp` or `exp(-k·dt)`), the trails allocate
    nothing, the showcases do not turn while the Playground is hidden, loads are memoised, the tint program is
    shared by all bodies, culling spheres are widened for the arms, the loop parks on a hidden tab.
